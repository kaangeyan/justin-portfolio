From Education to Digital Learning

Dr. Justin Sudhakar is an educator and researcher with extensive experience in teaching, academic research and digital learning. His academic journey has involved working with undergraduate, postgraduate and research-level learners.

Over time, his interest in educational technology led him to explore ways of transforming academic knowledge, teaching materials and research resources into accessible digital applications.

Today, his work brings together education, research, EdTech, digital content creation and examination-related technology.
