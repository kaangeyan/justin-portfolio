Teaching & Courses

Teaching Knowledge. Designing Learning. Building Digital Experiences.

Teaching has been a central part of Dr. Justin Sudhakar's academic journey, spanning undergraduate, postgraduate and research-level learning.

Teaching as a foundation

Teaching has provided a foundation for much of Dr. Justin Sudhakar's academic and digital work. Working with learners at different levels has involved not only delivering academic content, but also organizing knowledge, developing learning resources and exploring ways to make complex topics easier to understand.

This experience has gradually extended into digital learning, with selected courses, research topics and educational resources being transformed into Android and desktop applications.
