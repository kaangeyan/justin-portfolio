Ideas Into Applications. Knowledge Into Digital Experiences.

Exploring ways to transform educational knowledge, research resources and practical learning needs into accessible digital applications.

Building at the Intersection of Education & Technology

Teaching and research have provided many of the ideas behind the digital projects in this portfolio. Some applications organize academic knowledge, some support research and learning, while others explore practical educational and administrative workflows.

Praxis

Research Progress Tracker

Knowledge in Motion.

A research progress tracker designed for Arts and Social Sciences scholars.

Praxis comes pre-loaded with a standard research proposal structure and thesis chapters used in Arts and Social Sciences programmes. The content is editable to match institutional requirements.

Research Education Mobile Application
View on Google Play

For Researchers

  • Calendar — mark key dates and set reminders
  • Guide Meet — log advisor meetings with notes and follow-up reminders
  • Research — Proposal, Thesis and Follow-up stages
  • Timeline — weekly, monthly or half-yearly

For Advisors

  • Manage unlimited PhD and postgraduate students
  • Independent Calendar, Guide Meet, Research and Timeline for each student
  • Student cards showing chapter progress and last meeting date
  • Student photos and nicknames
